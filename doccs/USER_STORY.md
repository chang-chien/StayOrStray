# User Stories

## Scenario 1: Student deciding to stay in UK

As a student,
I want to compare UK vs Taiwan income over 10 years,
so I can decide whether to stay or return.

## Scenario 2: Graduate working in UK

As a graduate,
I want to understand my net income after tax, NI, pension,
so I can plan my monthly budget.

## Scenario 3: Early career professional

As a professional,
I want to simulate salary growth and savings potential,
so I can estimate financial independence timeline.

## Scenario 4: Housing planning (future)
As a user,
I want to estimate when I can afford a house in UK,
so I can plan long-term finances.
